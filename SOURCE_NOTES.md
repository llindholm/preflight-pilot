Phase 5 treats custom millwork release-package consistency as a small read-only test, not a proven market opportunity. It distinguishes approval/version/scope interpretation from CAD, field fit, engineering and fabrication authorization. Synthetic success does not establish prevalence, economics or commercial effectiveness. The page carries these boundaries forward without presenting the research as customer evidence.

PR-01 dated 22 September shows 3½ inches and is pending review; it is not an actual fabrication authorization. The page's example uses these supplied synthetic details and labels them accordingly. The field sheet explicitly states that field observations do not approve shop-drawing changes or establish fit.

The four finding labels and historical-only pilot scope come from the user's current brief. No quantified accuracy or benchmark-result claim was added. An unsupported toe-kick change is displayed as CONFLICT, not an automatic HOLD command; the suggested follow-up remains with a human. The production diagram is illustrative and not to scale.
